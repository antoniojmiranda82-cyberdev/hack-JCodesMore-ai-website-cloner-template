## Capture Record
Record the capture date and the browser/viewport used whenever a fresh reference pass is made.

## Sections

## Interaction Notes
Record scroll behavior, hover states, transitions, pinned sections, responsive changes, and other observable interactions.

## WebGL / Canvas Limitations
Canvas and WebGL-rendered content may not expose usable DOM styles to Dembrandt. Treat those areas as visual references and document any reconstructed behavior separately.
